McDonald’s Monopoly 2026: Game Dates, Rules and Prizes

There is no separate menu price for the Monopoly game. The official 2026 rules say no purchase is necessary and a purchase will not improve the chance of winning. Menu prices apply only to the individual food or drink selected, and those amounts vary by restaurant and order method. The homepage is a local price guide, not a fixed game entry price.

What the 2026 game is

Monopoly at McDonald’s is a limited time sweepstakes and app game. The current U.S. promotion page describes prizes across categories such as cash, travel, technology, sports and food. It is not a meal, sandwich or beverage, so the game itself has no ingredient list, calories or allergen profile. Each food item has its own product information.

To take part, eligible customers need to opt in to MyMcDonald’s Rewards and register for the game in the app. Follow the official rules for game pieces, app play, entry limits and prize claims. Prize availability and odds can change as prizes are claimed, so use the official rules rather than a recycled promotion calendar.

Dates and eligibility

The official promotion page says the 2026 U.S. game is open to residents of the fifty states and Washington, D.C. who are at least eighteen and meet the registration requirements. The dates below reflect the current official information checked on October 25, 2026. Do not assume a game piece or offer stays available after its stated window.

Purchases of eligible items can earn game pieces during the published period, subject to participation and supply limits. The official page also says only large beverages qualify for game play. That is a promotion rule, not a statement that every large drink is offered at every restaurant.

Purchase and food details are separate

No purchase is required under the official rules, but the alternate entry method has its own instructions and deadlines. A purchase does not improve the stated odds. Register before an eligible order if you want to check whether the app awards the listed bonus Chance Card play. One account and one registration per person are subject to the stated limits.

If you choose food, check its individual menu listing for the current price, ingredients and nutrition. For example, the homepage lists a standard Big Mac at $6.72, while the official product panel lists 580 calories for that sandwich alone. The meal, a drink substitution or a customization has a different nutrition total.

Ingredients

The Big Mac example consists of two beef patties, a sesame seed bun, Big Mac sauce, American cheese, lettuce, pickles and onions. The official ingredient page lists wheat and sesame in the bun, egg and soy in the sauce, and milk and soy in the cheese. This is not an ingredient list for the Monopoly sweepstakes.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s Monopoly price?+

The game has no separate purchase price in the official rules. No purchase is necessary, and menu prices apply only to the food or drink selected.

How many calories are in McDonald’s Monopoly?+

The game itself has no calories. Food items have separate nutrition panels. A standard Big Mac sandwich, shown here only as an example, has 580 calories.

When does the 2026 Monopoly game end?+

The current official page lists November 1, 2026 as the end of the eligible purchase period, November 8 for in app game play and November 22 as the prize claim deadline.

Do I have to buy food to enter?+

The official rules say no purchase is necessary and a purchase will not improve the chance of winning. Follow the official alternate entry instructions and deadlines.

Which drinks qualify for game play?+

The official promotion page says only large beverages are eligible. Check the official game terms for the current list and local availability.
