What the shareables list covers

The homepage groups several larger orders under Shareables and Bundles. The list includes a cookie tote, two large fries, 40 piece Chicken McNuggets, a nuggets and fries combination, and two named sandwich packs. These choices serve different needs. A side bundle is not the same food as a chicken share box, and a pack price should not be used to infer the price of each individual component.

The homepage gives a price and calorie listing for each displayed shareable. Some package totals carry an asterisk, and the listing does not publish a component by component ingredient panel for every named pack. This page reproduces those homepage references without inventing the exact contents of a package when the source does not spell them out.

Portion size and the nutrition example

A 40 piece Chicken McNuggets order is a sizeable group portion. McDonald’s official product panel lists 1,650 calories for the nuggets alone. The 40 piece order with two large fries is listed separately on the homepage at 2,610 calories. Those are different entries, so do not apply the standalone nugget panel to the fries bundle.

Sauces are separate nutrition choices, and calories vary by flavor. If you are planning a group order, count sauces and sides separately rather than assuming the base item total includes them.

Ingredients and allergy planning

The official 40 piece product describes all white meat chicken coated with a wheat based breading and cooked in vegetable oil. Its current ingredient page lists wheat as a declared allergen. Fries are a separate component with their own wheat and milk statement, so adding fries changes the allergen picture for a shared order.

The table below applies only to the standalone 40 piece Chicken McNuggets. It does not cover the cookie tote, sandwich packs, sauces or the two fries bundle. Shared equipment and preparation areas can create contact between foods. Ask the restaurant to confirm the current information for every item in the order.

Ingredients

McDonald’s describes Chicken McNuggets as white meat chicken. The official 40 piece ingredient panel lists chicken, water, vegetable oils, enriched wheat flour, corn flour, vegetable starch, salt, leavening, spices and flavor ingredients. The official allergen statement names wheat. If fries are added, their separate component statement lists wheat and milk.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s shareables price range?+

The homepage guide ranges from $6.29 for two large fries to $22.39 for the Classic Big Mac Pack. Local menu prices may vary.

How many calories are in 40 piece Chicken McNuggets?+

The official product panel lists 1,650 calories for the 40 nuggets alone. Sauces and sides are separate.

How many calories are in the 40 piece nuggets and fries listing?+

The homepage lists 2,610 calories for 40 piece nuggets with two large fries. The standalone nugget panel is 1,650 calories.

Which allergens are listed for 40 piece McNuggets?+

The official ingredient statement lists wheat. Fries have a separate statement that lists wheat and milk.

Are the Classic sandwich packs fully itemized?+

The homepage lists a price and calorie total, but its package entry does not provide a component by component ingredient panel. Check the local order screen for exact contents.
