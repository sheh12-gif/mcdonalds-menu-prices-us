McDonald’s 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets: Price and Nutrition

The 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets order is a standalone chicken portion, separate from the meal that adds fries and a drink. The homepage guide lists $5.79. McDonald’s official product page lists 410 calories before a dipping sauce, and the complete nutrition panel below is for the nuggets alone.

What is included in a 10 piece order

The standalone 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets order contains ten breaded nuggets. It does not include fries, a drink or a meal side. The official product description says the nuggets are made with all white meat chicken, and the nutrition page lists 410 calories for the standard serving before a sauce is added.

This distinction is useful when comparing the product with a 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal. The meal adds medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola and has a combined official total of 1,000 calories. The standalone price and the meal price are different homepage references, so check the full item name before comparing the two orders.

Piece count, price and sauce

The homepage lists several piece counts, from four nuggets to a 40 piece order. The prices shown in the table are exact references from that menu snapshot, but individual restaurant prices can vary. A higher piece count is a larger serving, not a different ingredient recipe for which the 10 piece nutrition should be multiplied without checking the official panel.

Dipping sauces are a separate choice. McDonald’s states that sauces add 30 to 110 calories each, depending on which one you choose. If you order more than one sauce, account for each packet separately. The sauce can also have its own ingredients and allergen declarations, so review the specific dip rather than assuming it matches the nuggets.

Nutrition and ingredient details

The full panel below includes calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugars, sodium and selected vitamins and minerals. The standard product page is the source for those numbers. Because the order can be customized with sauce or paired with a side, this table should be read as a product specific reference rather than a complete meal total.

The official ingredient statement describes chicken, vegetable oil, enriched flour, corn flour, starches, salt, leavening, spices and natural flavors. It lists wheat in the allergen statement. If you have an allergy, also consider the selected sauce and ask the restaurant about current handling and shared preparation surfaces.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The official ingredient list begins with white boneless chicken and vegetable oil, followed by enriched flour, bleached wheat flour, yellow corn flour, starch, salt, leavening, spices and natural flavors. McDonald’s describes the chicken as all white meat. The breading is part of the finished product and is why the official allergen statement identifies wheat.

The 10 piece order is served without an automatic sauce in the nutrition panel. If you add a dip, use its own product page for ingredients and calories. A meal bundle also adds fries and a drink, and has its own combined nutrition facts. The product name and full serving should match the nutrition information you use.

Allergen information

The official allergen statement for the standalone nuggets names wheat. Other allergens are marked not listed when they are not named in the reviewed statement. The ingredient list names soybean oil, but McDonald’s published contains statement identifies wheat. Shared preparation and cooking equipment may still result in contact with other foods.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets price?+

The homepage guide lists $5.79 for the standalone 10 Piece order. Restaurant prices and participation may differ.

How many calories are in ten Chicken McNuggets?+

McDonald’s official product page lists 410 calories before adding sauce, fries or a drink.

Does the 10 Piece McNuggets order include fries and a drink?+

No. The standalone item is nuggets only. The separate meal adds medium fries and a medium drink.

How many calories does McNuggets sauce add?+

McDonald’s says dipping sauces add 30 to 110 calories each, depending on the sauce.

Which allergen is listed for Chicken McNuggets?+

The official standalone nugget allergen statement lists wheat. Confirm current details with the restaurant if you have an allergy.
