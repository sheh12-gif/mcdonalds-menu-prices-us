Ordering Guide

How to Order Online

Order through the McDonald’s app or website by choosing a restaurant, selecting current menu items, and checking the final total before checkout. Participating locations may offer mobile pickup, curbside pickup, counter service, drive through ordering, or McDelivery. You can also order in person at the counter or drive through.

Delivery availability, fees, prices, and menu choices can differ from in restaurant orders. Use the official McDonald’s U.S. restaurant locator to find locations, posted hours, drive through service, and other amenities.

McDonald’s does not publish an official secret menu. Customer suggested combinations are not standardized, so ask whether the ingredients are available and confirm the price before ordering.

Plan Your Visit

Breakfast and Lunch Hours

Breakfast service usually ends in the morning, and lunch items become available when a restaurant switches menus. The exact breakfast cutoff, lunch start, opening time, and drive through schedule are set locally and may vary by day.

Check the selected restaurant’s posted hours and menu in the official locator or app before you travel. Not every location is open around the clock or serves every item at every hour.
