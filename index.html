Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the McDonald's $5 Meal Deal in 2026? +

The $5 Meal Deal (part of the McValue™ platform) includes your choice of a McDouble® or McChicken® sandwich, a 4-piece Chicken McNuggets®, small World Famous Fries®, and a small soft drink for $5.00.

Does McDonald’s still serve All-Day Breakfast? +

No, standard McDonald’s restaurants discontinued All-Day Breakfast to speed up drive-thru kitchen operations. Breakfast items end at 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM at select locations on weekends, though McCafé coffees and bakery items remain available all day.

Why do McDonald’s prices vary slightly by location? +

Approximately 93% of McDonald’s restaurants are independently owned and operated by local franchisees. Each franchisee sets prices based on local ingredients, rent, and labor costs, and delivery orders via third-party apps typically cost 15%–20% more than in-store or official app pickup.

How are Single vs. Combo Meal prices structured on this page? +

Every menu card displays the base single item price in a bold red badge at the top, followed by an easy-to-read breakdown comparing single sandwich prices with Medium and Large Combo Meal prices right inside the card.
