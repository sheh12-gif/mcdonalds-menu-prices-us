McDonald’s Daily Double Meal Deal Price and Calories

The homepage guide lists the Daily Double Meal Deal at $6. The official McDonald’s offer page says the deal starts at $6 and notes that it is $7 in some areas. Treat the homepage amount as a guide reference, not a checkout guarantee. The standalone Daily Double burger has its own price and is not the same purchase as this bundle.

What comes in the deal

The current official listing combines one Daily Double burger, four Chicken McNuggets, a small World Famous Fries and a small soft drink. The burger has two beef patties, American cheese, shredded lettuce, slivered onions, mayonnaise and two tomato slices on a bun. This is more food than the sandwich by itself, so do not use the burger panel as the meal total.

The official 1,090 calorie figure is for the listed combination with a small Coca-Cola. Choosing a different drink or adding sauce changes the nutrition. McDonald’s says sauces can add 30 to 110 calories each. Availability is limited time and participation varies.

How to read the price and calorie estimate

The homepage price and the official price footnote are both useful context. The homepage gives a local guide amount of $6, while the official offer page calls it a starting price and says some areas charge $7. Taxes, delivery charges, service fees and delivery menu prices are separate. Confirm the exact deal and total in the participating restaurant’s menu or app.

The calorie table below is the complete official panel for the standard meal build. It is not a calorie estimate for an order with a different beverage, different sauce or other customizations. If you are comparing it with a standalone burger, compare like with like and include the nuggets, fries and drink in the meal total.

Ingredients

The bundle is made from a Daily Double burger, four breaded Chicken McNuggets, a small serving of fries and a small fountain drink. The burger components include beef, a wheat bun, American cheese, mayonnaise, tomato, onion and lettuce. The official component statements list wheat in the bun, nuggets and fries, milk and soy in the cheese, egg in mayonnaise, and milk in the fries.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

What is the McDonald’s Daily Double Meal Deal price?+
