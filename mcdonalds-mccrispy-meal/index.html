McDonald’s McCrispy Meal: Price and Nutrition

The McCrispy Meal pairs the McCrispy chicken sandwich with medium World Famous Fries and a medium Sprite in the standard official build. The homepage reference is $12.59. McDonald’s lists 1,040 calories for that combination, and the full table below is not the nutrition total for the sandwich alone.

What comes with the McCrispy Meal

The standard McCrispy Meal includes the McCrispy chicken sandwich, medium World Famous Fries and a medium Sprite. McDonald’s describes the sandwich as southern style fried chicken on a toasted, buttered potato roll with crinkle cut pickles. The meal page names the side and drink used for its combined nutrition panel.

The sandwich alone and the meal are different products. The homepage guide lists a standalone McCrispy price of $4.99 and a separate meal reference of $12.59. The meal total includes fries and a beverage, so do not compare its calories with the sandwich only or interpret the sandwich price as covering the full combo.

How the 1,040 calorie total is calculated

The official page lists 1,040 calories for the meal with medium fries and a medium Sprite. Changing to another drink or fry size changes the build. A fountain drink’s calories can also depend on fill level and whether ice is used, as McDonald’s explains in its nutrition information. The listed number is for the standard combination, not every possible order.

The complete nutrition panel below is taken from the meal page, which directly lists the combined protein, carbohydrate, fat, sugars, sodium and micronutrient amounts. This is more reliable than adding rounded values from separate pages. If you customize the sandwich or replace the side, check the official calculator or updated product page for the new configuration.

Ingredients and meal planning

The crispy chicken fillet and potato roll are the main parts of the sandwich. Crinkle cut pickles add a sharp contrast, while the meal side and beverage make up a substantial share of its carbohydrates and sodium. McDonald’s publishes the ingredients for the standard sandwich and for the fries and Sprite separately as components of the meal.

The McCrispy Meal is one of several chicken meal options. A McCrispy, a Spicy McCrispy and the Deluxe version have different sandwich ingredients. If you are comparing prices or nutrition, match the exact variant and check whether the same drink and fry size are part of each order.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The sandwich is built with a crispy chicken fillet, potato roll, crinkle cut pickles and salted butter. The official product description calls the chicken southern style fried and served on a toasted, buttered potato roll. The meal adds medium fries and a medium Sprite. Each component has an ingredient statement, which is why the meal allergen profile includes more than the sandwich alone.

The fries contain potatoes and vegetable oil, and their official statement identifies wheat and milk. The roll is made with enriched wheat flour and may contain sesame. The Sprite ingredients include carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, citric acid, natural flavors and sodium benzoate. Use the product page for the full supplier statements and any current substitutions.

Allergen information

The standard McCrispy Meal component statements list wheat and milk, and the potato roll may contain sesame. Other major allergens are not named in the reviewed statements. Soybean oil appears in ingredient information, but the official declared allergen status follows the published contains and may contain wording. Shared preparation may cause contact with other foods.
