What is included in a Happy Meal

The official U.S. Happy Meal menu lists Hamburger, 4 piece Chicken McNuggets and 6 piece McNuggets versions. The standard box pairs an entree with kid sized fries, apple slices, a beverage choice and a toy. The toy is not part of the meal’s nutrition panel. Restaurant participation and toy availability can vary.

The published calorie total depends on the drink used for the calculation. The official 4 piece panel uses four McNuggets, kid fries, apple slices and a 1 percent low fat milk carton for 395 calories. Choosing water, juice or reduced sugar chocolate milk changes the nutrition, so use the selected beverage’s panel when comparing an actual order.

A practical way to compare choices

The homepage reference shows the Hamburger Happy Meal and 6 piece McNuggets Happy Meal at 475 calories in the standard listed build, while the 4 piece version is 395. That difference reflects the entree and serving choice, not a claim that all custom orders have those totals.

Apple slices are included in the described meal, and milk is one of the drink options. Parents can check the official kids nutrition page for current information about children’s meals and use the local order screen to see the available toy, drink and price. The calorie panel below is for the specific four piece build only.

Ingredients and allergens in the example build

The four piece meal contains breaded chicken nuggets, kid sized fries, apple slices and a 1 percent milk carton. The current component statements list wheat for the nuggets and fries, and milk for the fries and milk carton. The milk drink is a key reason the featured build’s allergen table differs from the nuggets alone.

The official meal page does not list a separate soy Contains declaration for the selected combination, although soybean oil appears in component ingredient details. This guide reports the component declarations as published and does not treat “Not listed” as an allergy safety guarantee. Shared equipment can also create cross contact.

Ingredients

The featured Happy Meal includes four all white meat Chicken McNuggets, kid sized fries, apple slices and a 1 percent milk carton. The nugget coating contains wheat, fries are prepared with potato and oil ingredients, the milk carton contains milk, and the apple side is apples with a freshness agent. The toy is a nonfood extra and has no calorie or allergen panel.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s Happy Meal price?+

The homepage guide lists $5.99 for a Hamburger Happy Meal, $6.93 for four piece McNuggets and $7.19 for six piece McNuggets. Check your restaurant for its current amount.

How many calories are in a 4 piece McNuggets Happy Meal?+

The official panel lists 395 calories with kid fries, apple slices and a 1 percent milk carton.

What comes in a Happy Meal?+

The official menu lists a hamburger or Chicken McNuggets entree, kid sized fries, apple slices, a beverage choice and a toy.

Does changing the drink change calories?+

Yes. The published 395 calorie figure uses a 1 percent milk carton. Water, juice and other drink choices have different nutrition.

Which allergens are listed for the example meal?+

The official component statements list wheat and milk for the featured build. Check the drink and current restaurant panel for the exact order.
