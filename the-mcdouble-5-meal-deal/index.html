McDonald’s McDouble $5 Meal Deal: Price and Calories

The McDouble $5 Meal Deal combines a McDouble, four Chicken McNuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola in the standard official build. The homepage guide lists $5, and McDonald’s lists 990 calories for the bundle. The full table below applies to this meal deal, not a standalone McDouble.

What the $5 bundle includes

The official McDouble Meal Deal page describes a McDouble, four Chicken McNuggets, small World Famous Fries and a small Coca-Cola. The homepage guide lists the bundle at $5. That combination is the basis for the 990 calorie official panel, so the calorie figure belongs to the complete deal rather than to any one component.

The standalone McDouble is another menu item, listed at $2.99 in the homepage guide and 390 calories on its official product page. It does not include nuggets, fries or a drink. The difference in product format is important when comparing a burger price with a meal deal price.

How to check deal terms

McDonald’s describes the meal deal as limited time and says prices and participation may vary. A $5 guide reference may not be shown at every restaurant, and delivery menu prices can be higher. Check that the deal appears for the specific restaurant and order channel before relying on the displayed amount.

The current ordering screen also confirms the included side, drink and available substitutions. Do not assume an optional sauce is included unless it appears in the offer details. Taxes, delivery fees and service charges may be separate. The final amount is the one shown at checkout for the selected location.

Why the full nutrition panel matters

The McDouble Meal Deal has 990 calories in the official panel. Its protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugars and sodium are not the same as the standalone McDouble values because the bundle includes three additional components. Ingredients and how the item is built

The bundle combines a McDouble with four breaded Chicken McNuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola. The burger includes two beef patties, a bun, American cheese, pickles, onions, ketchup and mustard. The nuggets and fries have separate ingredient statements, and the drink in the nutrition build is a small Coca-Cola.

The total meal nutrition includes the four nuggets and side items. The standalone McDouble panel is not a substitute for the bundle table. The same is true for the McChicken Meal Deal, which uses a different sandwich and has its own nutrient profile even though its official calorie total is also 990.

Allergen information

For the standard McDouble deal, component statements list wheat, milk and soy, and the burger bun may contain sesame. The nugget and fries components add their own wheat and milk information.
