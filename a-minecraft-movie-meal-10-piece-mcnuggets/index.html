McDonald’s A Minecraft Movie Meal with 10 Piece McNuggets Price: Calories and Allergens

The McDonald’s Minecraft Movie Meal with 10 Piece McNuggets price is not listed in the current guide. No official historical price or complete calorie panel was found for the promotion.

Price and current status

The McDonald’s Minecraft Movie Meal with 10 Piece McNuggets price is not listed in the current homepage menu. The homepage lists the regular 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal at $10.09, but that standard meal is not the same as the former themed bundle. McDonald’s official announcement confirms the promotion but does not give a historical U.S. price. Check the current menu guide and restaurant app for available items.

The former promotion paired the meal with a collectible. Bundle prices could differ by restaurant, and delivery totals can also include fees. A past price should not be used to estimate a new promotion without a current menu listing.

What the former bundle included

McDonald’s official March 2025 announcement describes a Big Mac or 10 piece Chicken McNuggets choice, medium fries, a drink, and a limited edition collectible. The McNuggets option also included Nether Flame Sauce. The announcement does not identify the drink or publish a full nutrition panel, so the former promotion should not be treated as identical to a standard meal today.

Ingredients

The list below names the main components reported for the former bundle. It does not reproduce every supplier subingredient, and the themed sauce is not part of the current standard meal listing.

  • 10 Chicken McNuggets: white meat chicken in a wheat based coating.
  • Medium fries: potatoes, vegetable oil, seasoning, and salt.
  • Drink: the official announcement does not specify the historical flavor or size.
  • Nether Flame Sauce: an official limited edition hot sauce described as including crushed red pepper and cayenne, with garlic and sweetness. A complete archived ingredient statement was not available.
  • Collectible: one themed toy was included while the promotion lasted.

That is a comparison, not the former Minecraft build, which included a drink choice and Nether Flame Sauce. Frequently asked questions

These answers distinguish the older promotional bundle from current standard menu items.

What is the McDonald’s Minecraft Meal price?+

This themed meal is not listed in the current homepage guide. The regular 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal is $10.09, but it is not the themed collectible bundle. The official announcement does not provide a historical U.S. price.

How many calories were in the 10 Piece McNuggets Minecraft Meal?+

No complete official calorie panel for the former themed bundle was found. The current standard 10 Piece meal with medium Coca-Cola is 1,000 calories, but it is a different combination.

What came with the 10 Piece McNuggets version?+

McDonald’s official announcement confirms the 10 piece McNuggets choice, medium fries, a drink, Nether Flame Sauce, and one limited edition collectible. It does not specify the drink flavor or size in its U.S. description.

Which allergens should I check?+

Can I still order the Minecraft Movie Meal?+

It was a limited time promotion. The current homepage guide does not list it, so check the official app or restaurant for any local return.
