McDonald’s McValue Menu Deals: Prices and What Is Included

McValue brings together breakfast offers, lunch and dinner meal deals, and selected lower priced items. The homepage guide lists meal deal references from $4 to $6. The featured full nutrition panel is for the McDouble Meal Deal, a specific bundle with 990 calories, not a nutrition total for every McValue offer.

How McValue groups its offers

The official McValue category includes breakfast meal deals, lunch and dinner bundles, and an Under $3 menu. Those groups serve different purposes. A breakfast offer is available during breakfast hours, while a lunch or dinner deal has a different sandwich and sides. The category page is the clearest way to see which named promotions are active in the current menu snapshot.

The homepage guide used here lists two breakfast deals at $4, McChicken and McDouble bundles at $5, and the Daily Double bundle at $6. These amounts are useful reference points, but McDonald’s says offers are limited time and participation varies. A deal shown in one area may not appear in another, and delivery prices can be higher.

What to check before choosing a bundle

A meal deal is not simply the sandwich at a reduced price. The McDouble bundle, for example, includes one McDouble, four Chicken McNuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola in the official standard build. The McChicken bundle uses a McChicken in place of the McDouble. Read the exact contents because another deal can include different sides, a breakfast item or a different drink.

Check whether the price shown applies to the restaurant, app or delivery order you are placing. Taxes, delivery fees and local menu prices may change the final total. If an item is out of stock, do not assume a substitution is automatically included at the same price. Confirm the offer conditions and substitutions in the local order screen.

Why each meal needs its own nutrition panel

The $5 McDouble Meal Deal has 990 calories in the official panel. The McChicken Meal Deal also lists 990 calories, but its amounts for protein, carbohydrates, fats and sodium differ. The Daily Double bundle is another build with its own total. A shared headline price does not mean the bundles have identical ingredients or nutrition.

The table below gives the full official panel for the McDouble version. It applies to the standard McDouble, four piece nuggets, small fries and small Coca-Cola combination. A different drink, side, sauce or customization changes the order. Use the official page for the specific bundle rather than adding rounded values from standalone products.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The official McDouble Meal Deal page describes a McDouble, four Chicken McNuggets, small World Famous Fries and a small Coca-Cola. The burger includes beef patties, a bun, American cheese, pickles, onions, ketchup and mustard. The nuggets are breaded chicken pieces, the fries are potatoes prepared in vegetable oil, and the drink is a fountain beverage.

The full bundle has more components than the McDouble sandwich alone. Its calorie and allergen information therefore combines the declared ingredients for the burger, nuggets, fries and drink. A change to any component can change the nutrition or allergen status, so use the meal page for the standard build and the local menu for substitutions.

Allergen information

For the standard McDouble Meal Deal, component statements list wheat, milk and soy. The McDouble bun may contain sesame. The meal also includes nuggets and fries, which have their own ingredient declarations. Not listed means the reviewed statement did not name that allergen. Shared preparation can still cause contact with other foods.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McValue menu deal price?+

The homepage guide lists selected offers from $4 to $6. McDonald’s says offers are limited time and prices and participation vary by location.

How many calories are in the McDouble Meal Deal?+

The official standard build has 990 calories with a McDouble, four nuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola.

What comes with the McDouble $5 Meal Deal?+

The official bundle includes a McDouble, four Chicken McNuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola in the nutrition panel build.

Are McValue offers available at every restaurant?+

No. McDonald’s says participation and prices may vary. Check the current local menu for active offers and terms.

Are all McValue meal deals 990 calories?+

No. The McChicken Meal Deal also lists 990 calories, but other deals have different builds and nutrition. Use the official panel for the exact offer.
