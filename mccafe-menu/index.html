What is on the McCafé menu

The official McCafé category separates coffee and classics from specialty drinks. The menu includes brewed Premium Roast and decaf coffee, Americano, latte, cappuccino, caramel macchiato, mocha latte, iced coffee, iced espresso drinks, frappés and hot chocolate. The category is broader than espresso alone, so compare the recipe and size before ordering.

Plain brewed coffee and an espresso Americano have small calorie totals in the homepage guide. Milk based lattes and cappuccinos add calories from milk. Flavored syrups, chocolate, whipped topping and caramel drizzle add more. The nutrition values below describe one standard small whole milk latte, not a customized specialty drink.

Hot, iced and blended choices

A hot latte combines espresso with steamed milk. An iced latte uses espresso and milk over ice. A mocha adds chocolate syrup and may include whipped cream and drizzle, while a frappé is blended and typically has a dessert style texture. The official pages list sizes and nutrition separately, which is why one drink name can have several calorie totals.

Use the local ordering screen to compare prices by size. Some flavor options or sugar free syrups may be available only at participating locations. Adding a flavor or changing the milk can alter the original panel, so treat the published standard recipe as a baseline rather than a custom order calculation.

Ingredients and allergen information

The featured small latte is made with whole milk, water and espresso. Its official ingredient statements list milk for the whole milk component. It does not have the same ingredients as a plain black coffee, and a flavored latte may add syrup or other ingredients.

“Not listed” in the allergen table means the current component statement reviewed does not name that allergen. It is not a statement that every drink is allergen free. Shared equipment and customizations can matter. Confirm the exact beverage and current restaurant information when an allergy is involved.

Nutrition information

The official small Latte page lists 140 calories when prepared with whole milk. The complete panel includes 8 grams of protein, 13 grams of carbohydrate and 7 grams of fat. A flavored syrup or a different size changes the drink from the standard panel shown here.

Ingredients

A standard small McCafé Latte is made from espresso, water and steamed whole milk. The official component statement lists milk in the milk ingredient. Other McCafé drinks can add chocolate syrup, caramel flavor, whipped topping, ice or different milk options, so do not apply the latte’s ingredient list to every coffee or frappé.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s McCafé menu price range?+

Selected homepage references range from $2.39 for small brewed coffee or an Americano to $3.59 for a small Mocha Latte. Other sizes and items cost more.

How many calories are in a small McCafé Latte?+

The official product page lists 140 calories for a small latte made with whole milk.

What is the difference between a latte and a mocha?+

A latte combines espresso and steamed milk. The official Mocha Latte page adds chocolate syrup and may include whipped topping and chocolate drizzle.

Does the McCafé menu have iced drinks?+

Yes. The official category lists iced coffee, iced lattes, iced mocha and other chilled choices, with nutrition varying by drink and size.

Which allergen is listed for a standard latte?+

The current standard latte component statement lists milk. Flavor additions and customizations have separate ingredient information.
