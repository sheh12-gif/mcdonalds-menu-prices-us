How Extra Value Meals are assembled

The official Extra Value Meals category spans breakfast meals and lunch or dinner combinations. A breakfast meal may pair an egg sandwich with Hash Browns and a small coffee. A lunch or dinner meal can pair a burger or chicken entree with fries and a selected soft drink. The exact contents depend on the item name and size.

An entree, side and drink each have separate ingredient statements, but the official meal page may also publish a combined nutrition panel for a standard build. The Big Mac Meal example below uses medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola. A different beverage or fry size changes the total. Do not substitute a standalone burger calorie figure for the meal.

Reading the price and nutrition table

The homepage provides guide prices for selected meal combinations. It also displays calorie references, and the table pairs listed prices with the standard calorie totals shown for each named combo. Local menu boards and the app determine the current offer at a restaurant.

Breakfast meal prices can look different from lunch and dinner prices because the entree and included side are different. Some restaurants may promote a limited time meal or a bundle alongside regular meal listings. Check the exact product name before comparing, especially when a deal is limited to the app or a participating location.

Ingredients and allergen notes for a Big Mac Meal

The Big Mac Meal includes the Big Mac sandwich, medium World Famous Fries and a medium beverage. The burger includes beef, bun, sauce, cheese, lettuce, pickles and onions. The official meal panel lists wheat, sesame, egg, soy and milk in the components, including wheat and milk in the fries.

The allergen chart below describes the standard listed combination. It does not cover a drink substitution, optional sauce or custom build. “Not listed” means the reviewed official component statement did not name the allergen. It does not guarantee that a food has no shared kitchen contact.

Ingredients

The Big Mac Meal combines a Big Mac sandwich, medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola. The sandwich includes two beef patties, a sesame seed bun, Big Mac sauce, American cheese, lettuce, pickles and onions. Fries are prepared with potatoes and vegetable oil, and the drink uses carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, caramel color, natural flavors and caffeine.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s Extra Value Meal price range?+

The selected homepage references range from $8.19 for an Egg McMuffin Meal to $11.29 for a Quarter Pounder with Cheese Meal. Local prices may differ.

How many calories are in a Big Mac Meal?+

The official standard build with medium fries and medium Coca-Cola has 1,170 calories.

What comes with a Big Mac Meal?+

The official page lists a Big Mac, medium World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink. The calorie panel uses medium Coca-Cola.

Are breakfast meals part of Extra Value Meals?+

Yes. The official category lists breakfast combinations such as Egg McMuffin Meal and Sausage McMuffin with Egg Meal, alongside lunch and dinner meals.

Which allergens are listed for a Big Mac Meal?+

The component statements list wheat, sesame, egg, soy and milk. Check the current ingredient panel for any customized order.
