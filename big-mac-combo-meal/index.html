McDonald’s Big Mac Combo Meal: Price and Nutrition

The Big Mac Combo Meal, listed by McDonald’s as the Big Mac Meal, combines the Big Mac with medium fries and a medium soft drink. The homepage price reference is $9.29. The official standard build with medium Coca-Cola has 1,170 calories, and its full nutrition appears below.

What is in the Big Mac Meal

The standard Big Mac Meal combines the Big Mac sandwich with medium World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink. The official calorie panel specifically uses medium Coca-Cola. The Big Mac itself is made with two beef patties, Big Mac sauce, a sesame seed bun, American cheese, pickles, shredded lettuce and chopped onions.

A standalone Big Mac is a different order from the combo. The homepage price guide lists $6.72 for the sandwich and $9.29 for the meal. The meal includes fries and a drink, so its price and nutrition should not be described using the burger alone. Check the exact item name on the local menu.

Ingredients and ordering choices

The Big Mac sauce, sesame seed bun and American cheese contribute allergen information that is not present in the drink. The medium fries have their own ingredient statement, and the Coca-Cola standard build lists carbonated water, high fructose corn syrup, caramel color, natural flavors and caffeine. The meal allergen table combines the components included in the official panel.

If you choose a different soft drink, add a sauce or change the sandwich, use the current product pages for those items. The listed price is a homepage reference and can differ at a restaurant. McDelivery can also have higher menu prices and separate delivery charges. The local order screen is the place to confirm the total.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The official Big Mac description identifies two beef patties, Big Mac sauce, a sesame seed bun, American cheese, pickles, shredded lettuce and chopped onion. The combo page then adds medium World Famous Fries and a medium Coca-Cola. Each part has its own supplier ingredient statement, and the combined nutrition panel reflects the named build.

Allergen information

The Big Mac Meal components list wheat, sesame, egg, soy and milk. The sesame seed bun and sauce have their own declarations, and the fries add wheat and milk. These statuses apply to the standard combo and its medium Coca-Cola. Shared preparation can still cause contact with other foods.
