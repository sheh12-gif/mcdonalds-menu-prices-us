Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit this guide or email us. It applies to mcdonaldsmenu-prices.us, not to third party websites linked from our pages.

Last reviewed October 25, 2026

Information You Provide

This site does not currently ask visitors to create accounts, submit a web form, or make a payment. If you email us, we receive the information included in your message, such as your email address, name if provided, and any details you choose to share.

We use that information to reply, review a correction or privacy request, and maintain a record when needed to resolve the matter.

Technical Information

When a page is requested, the hosting provider may process technical details such as an Internet Protocol address, requested page, date and time, browser type, device information, referring page, or error information. These details can be used to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the site. We do not operate a visitor account system.

The current site code does not intentionally run analytics or advertising scripts. See the Cookie Policy for details about cookies and similar technologies.

How Information Is Used

We use information to operate and secure the site, respond to messages, correct content, and meet applicable legal requirements. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Service Providers and Disclosure

Hosting or email providers may process information as needed to provide their services. We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the site, our users, or another person. We do not share message details for third party advertising.

Retention and Security

We keep email correspondence only for as long as it is useful to respond, document a correction, protect the site, or meet a legal obligation. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information by email.

Your Choices

You can choose not to email us. If you have sent a message and want to ask about access, correction, or deletion of the information in it, write to caroline@mcdonaldsmenu-prices.us. We will review requests under the laws that apply to us.

Children’s Privacy

This site is a general information guide and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

External Websites

Links to McDonald’s and other external websites are provided for reference. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Policy Changes

We may revise this policy when the site or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows when this copy was last reviewed.
