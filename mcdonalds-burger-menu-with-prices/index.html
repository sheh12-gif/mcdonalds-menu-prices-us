How to compare the burger lineup

The official U.S. burger category includes classic choices such as Hamburger and Cheeseburger, two patty options such as McDouble and Double Cheeseburger, and signature sandwiches such as Big Mac and the Quarter Pounder family. The Daily Double is a distinct build with tomato and mayonnaise. Chicken and fish sandwiches are listed in a separate official menu category.

Prices alone do not tell you whether a burger is a small snack or a full sandwich. Calories and protein differ by recipe, and additions such as cheese, bacon or sauce change the build. The price and calorie table below pairs selected homepage prices with calorie references for the named standalone sandwiches.

Fresh beef and classic burger differences

McDonald’s describes Quarter Pounder patties as fresh beef cooked when ordered, while several classic burgers use a standard beef patty. Bun size, toppings and patty count help explain why the menu spans a range of calories. A lower homepage price does not necessarily mean the item has fewer calories or less sodium.

If you want to compare a burger with a meal, account for the side and drink separately. A Big Mac sandwich is 580 calories on the official item page; the Big Mac Meal with medium fries and medium Coca-Cola is 1,170. The meal figure is not an alternate sandwich value.

Price and nutrition notes

Prices vary with restaurant, location and order channel. Delivery may carry higher prices and extra fees. This local guide uses homepage listings for the price examples below and current McDonald’s product information for the featured Daily Double panel.

For full nutrient and allergen detail on a particular burger, use that item’s current product page. The complete panel below features the Daily Double so the listed meal and sandwich values do not get mixed together. It is one useful example, not a substitute for a custom order calculation.

Ingredients

The Daily Double has two beef patties, American cheese, shredded lettuce, slivered onions, mayonnaise and two tomato slices in a regular bun. McDonald’s component details say the bun contains wheat and may contain sesame, cheese contains milk and soy, and mayonnaise contains egg. The other named ingredients are beef, lettuce, onions and tomato.

This summary describes the featured example only. Check the current official ingredient page for the complete statement, substitutions and customizations.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s burger menu price range?+

The selected homepage listings range from $2.99 for a McDouble to $8.99 for a Bacon Quarter Pounder with Cheese. Check the local menu for current prices.

What is the McDonald’s Big Mac price?+

The homepage guide lists $6.72 for the standalone Big Mac. Local restaurant and delivery prices can differ.

How many calories are in a Daily Double?+

The official product page lists 490 calories in the sandwich. The separate Daily Double Meal Deal is 1,090 calories with four nuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola.

How many calories are in a Big Mac?+

The official product page lists 580 calories for the Big Mac sandwich. A meal includes fries and a drink, so it has a different total.

What allergens are listed for a Daily Double?+

The component statements list wheat, milk, soy and egg. The bun may contain sesame. Check the latest statement and restaurant practices.
