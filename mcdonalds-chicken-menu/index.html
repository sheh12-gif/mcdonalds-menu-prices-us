McDonald’s Chicken Menu: Prices, Choices and Nutrition

McDonald’s chicken choices are split across sandwiches, Chicken McNuggets, McCrispy Strips and Snack Wraps. This guide uses exact prices shown on the homepage and a separate official nutrition panel for 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets. A sandwich, a piece count and a meal are different servings, so their totals should not be compared as if they were the same order.

A menu spanning handhelds and chicken pieces

The official menu separates chicken and fish sandwiches from McNuggets and McCrispy Strips. The sandwich group includes McChicken and several McCrispy builds, while the nugget section offers piece counts and the strip section offers a different breaded format. Snack Wraps are listed as their own category, with Ranch and Spicy versions available in the menu snapshot reviewed for this guide.

That structure helps when choosing by appetite. A sandwich is a single handheld entree, while a nugget order is portioned by count and may be paired with dipping sauces. Strips are larger chicken pieces, and a wrap adds a tortilla, toppings and sauce. The homepage price list reflects those different formats rather than a single fixed chicken meal price.

How to use the price references

The homepage reference for McChicken is $2.99, and the official product page reports 390 calories. The homepage also shows a different McChicken calorie entry. Because nutrition should follow the official product page, this guide keeps the price and calorie sources separate instead of repeating the discrepancy as settled data.

For a quick comparison, note whether the listed amount covers one sandwich, a count of nuggets or a meal with fries and a drink. A 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets order at $5.79 is not the same product as the 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal at $10.09. Meal pages name their sides and drink, which is why the bundle nutrition is higher.

Choosing chicken and checking nutrition

The breaded items can be ordered with dipping sauces, and McDonald’s notes that sauces add calories. A sauce is an extra item, not a reason to use the nutrition total for a different product. For a sandwich or meal, open that exact official page and check its standard build, especially when switching the drink, side or sauce.

This article gives the complete verified panel for the 10 piece nuggets as a consistent comparison point. It is useful for checking protein, sodium and other nutrients without implying that the whole chicken category shares one profile. Ingredients and allergen declarations differ among the bun, tortilla, sauce, chicken coating and sides, so review the product specific details for the order you plan to make.

Nutrition information

The official product page lists 410 calories for 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets. This is the nuggets alone, without a dipping sauce, fries or drink. McDonald’s says sauces add 30 to 110 calories each, depending on the selection.

Ingredients and how the item is built

McDonald’s describes Chicken McNuggets as tender pieces made with all white meat chicken. The official ingredient list also identifies water, vegetable oil, enriched flour, corn flour, starches, salt, leavening, spices and natural flavors. The outside coating is part of the product, so the chicken count alone does not describe the full ingredient list.

Sandwiches, strips and wraps have different components. McCrispy sandwiches use a breaded fillet with a roll and pickles, while a Snack Wrap adds a tortilla, shredded lettuce, cheese and a selected sauce. Check the official product page for the exact item because those ingredient lists should not be copied from the nuggets panel.

Allergen information

For the standard 10 piece nugget serving, the official allergen statement identifies wheat. Other entries below are marked not listed when the reviewed statement does not name them. The ingredient list names soybean oil, but the allergen status shown here follows McDonald’s declared contains statement. Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s chicken menu price?+

The homepage references in this guide range from $2.99 for a McChicken or Ranch Snack Wrap to $5.79 for 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets. Local prices vary.

How many calories are in 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets?+

The official page lists 410 calories for the nuggets without sauce. A dipping sauce adds calories, and McDonald’s lists a range of 30 to 110 per sauce.

What types of chicken items are on the menu?+

The official categories include chicken sandwiches, Chicken McNuggets, McCrispy Strips and Snack Wraps. Each format has its own ingredients and serving size.

Is the McChicken 390 or 400 calories?+

The official McChicken product page says 390 calories. The homepage snapshot has a different figure, so this guide uses the official product panel for nutrition.

Which allergens are listed for 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets?+

The official allergen statement lists wheat. Always check the current product information and ask the restaurant about shared preparation if an allergy is involved.

McDonald’s chicken menu includes several distinct formats, and the price or nutrition depends on the item and serving size. Use the homepage references as a guide, then check the official product page for the exact sandwich, nugget count, wrap or meal you are ordering.
