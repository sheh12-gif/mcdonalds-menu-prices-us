Cookie Policy

This policy explains cookies and similar technologies used by this guide. The current site is a static information website without visitor accounts or online checkout.

Last reviewed October 25, 2026

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files a website can store in a browser. Similar technologies can store or read information on a device. They are often used for essential site functions, preferences, analytics, or advertising.

What This Site Uses

The current site code does not intentionally create or read cookies for analytics, advertising, or visitor profiles. Menu search and category filtering run in the page and do not require a sign in or account.

The hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. We do not control any cookies or similar technology that the hosting provider may use for its infrastructure.

External Websites

When you follow a link to McDonald’s or another third party website, that site may use its own cookies or similar tools. We do not control those technologies. Review the external site’s privacy and cookie information before continuing.

Your Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. Browser controls can affect how outside websites work, but they do not change the information already shown on this guide.

Policy Changes

If the site begins using cookies or similar technologies for a new purpose, we will update this page to explain the change. For privacy questions, email caroline@mcdonaldsmenu-prices.us or read the Privacy Policy.
