Contact Us

This website is an independent menu information guide. For questions about our pages, corrections, or privacy requests, contact us by email.

Last reviewed October 25, 2026

Email Us

Write to caroline@mcdonaldsmenu-prices.us. We do not currently provide a contact form or customer support for McDonald’s orders.

Send a Correction

For a possible error, include the page address, the menu item or statement, the restaurant or area if relevant, and a current source that supports the correction. For a price issue, include when and how the price was shown, such as in the app or at a restaurant.

What We Can Help With

  • Corrections to information published on this guide
  • Questions about our editorial approach or sources
  • Accessibility feedback about the website
  • Privacy requests concerning an email you sent to us

We cannot change McDonald’s prices, offers, restaurant hours, ingredients, or order status. For current restaurant information, contact the restaurant or use McDonald’s official app and website.

Email and Privacy

Please do not email passwords, payment details, health records, or other sensitive information. We use the details in your message to respond and handle the request. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

We do not promise a particular response time. Messages are reviewed as time allows.
