What the value menu means in 2026

McDonald’s current official menu uses the name Under $3 for a selected group of lower priced items. It does not promise that the menu has a separate one dollar, two dollar and three dollar section in every restaurant. The exact lineup can change, and breakfast choices are not interchangeable with lunch choices because the restaurant serves them at different times.

The official list includes a mix of breakfast foods, a small coffee and familiar lunch sides or sandwiches. In the homepage snapshot used for this guide, the highlighted entries run from $2.49 to $2.99. Those amounts are reference prices only. Taxes, delivery charges, special offers and the selected restaurant can change the total shown at checkout.

How to compare the prices and calories

A listed price is not a calorie statement. The homepage guide supplies the price references, while the nutrition values below come from the official McDouble product page. Keeping those sources separate matters because the McChicken row on the homepage has a different calorie figure from its official product page. This article does not repeat that disputed homepage calorie value as settled nutrition data.

The McDouble is a useful example because it is available as a standalone sandwich and as part of a meal deal. The $2.99 reference applies to the sandwich, not the larger bundle. A side, drink or added sauce changes the order total and nutrition. Compare the same serving and product format before deciding which price represents better value.

Choosing a small order

For a quick snack, one lower priced sandwich or a small side keeps the order simple. A breakfast item makes sense only during breakfast service, while McDouble, McChicken and McNuggets are lunch or dinner choices. If you want a full meal, compare the current McValue bundle separately instead of assuming that fries and a drink are included in an Under $3 listing.

If nutrition is part of the decision, look at the official page for the exact item and serving. The featured McDouble table below gives calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, sugars, sodium and micronutrients. For another product, use its own official panel rather than borrowing the McDouble numbers or treating a price category as a nutrition category.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The standard McDouble combines two beef patties, a regular bun, American cheese, pickles, chopped onions, ketchup and mustard. Its familiar construction is compact, but the cheese and bun contribute important allergen information. The ingredient list is more specific than a short menu description, so check the current McDonald’s product page if you need the complete supplier statement.

The McDouble sold on its own is distinct from the McDouble Meal Deal. The bundle adds four Chicken McNuggets, small fries and a small Coca-Cola. Do not transfer the sandwich nutrition table to the bundle. Each format has a separate official panel and a different total.

Allergen information

The official McDouble product statement identifies wheat, milk and soy as contains allergens and notes that sesame may be present. The table refers to the sandwich by itself. McDonald’s also warns that shared cooking and preparation areas can create contact with other foods, so a not listed status is not an allergen free claim.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s $1, $2 and $3 menu price?+

The current homepage references include selected items from $2.49 to $2.99. Local restaurant prices can differ, and the current official category calls these Under $3 choices.

How many calories are in a McDouble?+

The official McDouble product page lists 390 calories for the standard sandwich. This figure does not include fries, a drink or a meal deal.

Which items are under three dollars in the homepage guide?+

Examples include McDouble, McChicken, four piece Chicken McNuggets, small fries, Sausage Biscuit, Sausage Burrito and Hash Browns.

Does every restaurant offer the same Under $3 menu?+

No. McDonald’s says prices and participation may vary. Check the menu for the specific restaurant and time of day.

Which allergens are listed for a McDouble?+

The official statement lists wheat, milk and soy, and says sesame may be present. Shared preparation can also cause contact with other foods.

Conclusion
