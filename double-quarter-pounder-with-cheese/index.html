Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese Price: Calories and Allergens

The Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese price here is $6.99. One sandwich has 740 calories, with nutrition, ingredients, and allergen notes below.

Price and menu status

The Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese price shown in the homepage menu is $6.99 for one sandwich. In the same guide, that is 60 cents above the standard Quarter Pounder with Cheese listing. Prices at a local counter or delivery service can be different, and the reference does not include tax, sides, drinks, fees, or custom additions. Check the full menu guide for the other listed item estimates, then use the selected restaurant’s live total before paying.

All price figures on this page refer to the sandwich alone unless a meal is explicitly named. They are comparison points, not a promise that every restaurant uses the same price.

What to expect from the Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese

This version keeps the familiar Quarter Pounder toppings but uses two beef patties, each measured at 4 ounces before cooking. That is an 8 ounce combined pre cooking weight, not the cooked weight on the tray. McDonald’s describes the Quarter Pounder patties as fresh beef cooked when ordered at most restaurants in the contiguous states, with regional exceptions. Two cheese slices, ketchup, mustard, pickles, and onions complete the standard sandwich.

Ingredients

The components below describe one standard U.S. sandwich. Ingredient brands and preparation may change, so use the linked official product page for the latest full ingredient statement.

  • Quarter Pound bun: sesame seed bun with wheat ingredients.
  • Two beef patties: 4 ounces each before cooking, seasoned with salt and pepper.
  • American cheese: two slices.
  • Ketchup.
  • Mustard.
  • Pickle slices.
  • Slivered onions.
