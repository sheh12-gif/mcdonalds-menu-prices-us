McDonald’s Big Breakfast with Hotcakes: Price and Calories

The Big Breakfast with Hotcakes combines sweet and savory breakfast foods in one platter. The homepage guide lists $8.29, and the official McDonald’s product page consistently lists 1,340 calories for the standard serving. The nutrition table below covers the item with Hotcakes, syrup and butter, not the plain Big Breakfast.

What comes with the platter

The official product description names a warm biscuit, savory sausage, scrambled eggs, Hash Browns and golden Hotcakes. The ingredient carousel also lists Hotcake Syrup and butter components. This mix gives the platter both sweet and savory parts. The standard item is larger than Hotcakes by themselves, and its nutrition panel should be used for the whole platter.

The Big Breakfast with Hotcakes is different from the plain Big Breakfast, which does not include Hotcakes in its description. Their prices, contents and calories should not be combined. The homepage guide lists separate price references for the two. The product name in the order screen helps confirm which platter is included.

A sweet and savory breakfast in one

Hotcakes add a sweet element to a plate that already includes eggs, sausage, a biscuit and Hash Browns. If you are choosing between this platter and a smaller breakfast item, consider whether you want the full combination rather than simply comparing the price of pancakes. The official product page is the best source for the standard serving details.

The ingredients and allergens come from several components. Hotcakes list wheat, egg, milk and soy. Biscuit and Hash Browns also contribute wheat and milk, while the sausage patty has a different statement. The meal table below summarizes the overall declared allergens for the standard item and notes that shared preparation can still create contact.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The standard platter includes Hotcakes, scrambled eggs, a biscuit, Hotcake Syrup, Hash Browns, a sausage patty and butter components. The official ingredient carousel identifies each component separately. Hotcakes include enriched wheat flour, whey, corn flour, soybean oil, sugar and eggs, while the sausage patty is made from pork with water, salt, spices and other ingredients.

The biscuit, Hash Browns and butter each have their own ingredient declarations. This is why the total allergen status reflects several components rather than only the pancakes. The official page is the source for the current complete supplier statements and any modifications to the standard platter.

Allergen information

The standard Big Breakfast with Hotcakes ingredient statements list wheat, egg, milk and soy. Other major allergens are marked not listed in the reviewed component declarations. No sesame may contain statement is shown for this item, but shared preparation may still cause contact. Check current McDonald’s information if an allergy is involved.
