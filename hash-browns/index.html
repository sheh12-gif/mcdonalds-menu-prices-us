McDonald’s Hash Browns Price: Calories and Allergens

The McDonald’s Hash Browns price in this guide is $2.69. One serving has 140 calories, with its full nutrition and ingredient details below.

Price and breakfast menu status

The McDonald’s Hash Browns price shown on this homepage is $2.69 for one side. Treat it as a local guide reference, not a nationwide quote. Restaurant pricing, delivery charges, taxes, and limited offers can change the total, so confirm the current amount in the app or at your restaurant. Hash Browns are a breakfast side rather than a complete meal, and the price does not include a sandwich or drink.

McDonald’s lists Hash Browns among its breakfast value options, while the homepage price is the reference used here. To compare other breakfast items, visit the breakfast menu guide or return to the full homepage menu.

What are McDonald’s Hash Browns?

Hash Browns are a single shredded potato patty cooked until crisp outside and soft inside. They are often paired with a breakfast sandwich or coffee, but they can also be ordered on their own. Because the portion is one compact side, its 140 calories are lower than many breakfast sandwiches, although the hash brown still contributes fat and sodium. Add a beverage or sandwich only if you want a larger breakfast, since those additions change the meal’s nutrition and total price.

Ingredients

The current official ingredient statement lists potatoes, vegetable oil made with canola and soybean oils, salt, corn flour, dehydrated potato, dextrose, sodium acid pyrophosphate to maintain color, black pepper extractives, and natural beef flavor. McDonald’s says the natural beef flavor contains wheat and milk derivatives. This is more than plain potato, and recipes can change, so check the latest restaurant information when ingredients matter.

The ingredient panel does not make a vegetarian or gluten free claim. In particular, the natural beef flavor and wheat derivatives are important details for people following those diets.

Allergen information

McDonald’s lists wheat and milk as contained allergens in its current Hash Browns statement. The table distinguishes those declarations from allergens not named in the statement.
