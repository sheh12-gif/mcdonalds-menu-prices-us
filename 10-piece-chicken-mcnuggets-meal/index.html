McDonald’s 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal: Price and Nutrition

The 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal pairs ten nuggets with medium World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink. The homepage guide lists $10.09, and McDonald’s official standard build lists 1,000 calories with medium Coca-Cola. The full nutrition table below is for that complete meal, not for ten nuggets by themselves.

What the meal includes

The standard meal consists of ten Chicken McNuggets, a medium serving of World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink. McDonald’s official nutrition page uses medium Coca-Cola for its 1,000 calorie total. The beverage matters because a different drink can change the calorie and sugar values, while a different fry size changes the side portion.

The standalone 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets order is a separate menu item. The homepage price reference for the meal is $10.09, while the nuggets by themselves are listed at $5.79. The meal price does not mean the included side or drink can be removed for the same amount. Check the local ordering screen for any available substitutions.

How to read the nutrition total

The 1,000 calorie total belongs to the complete standard build. It is not the calorie count for the nuggets alone, and it does not include an extra dipping sauce. McDonald’s states that sauces add 30 to 110 calories each, depending on the sauce. That separate addition can matter when comparing an order with a different dip or drink.

The meal table below includes protein, carbohydrates, total and saturated fat, sugars, sodium and micronutrients. These values come from the official meal page rather than by adding rounded numbers from separate product pages. That matters because a combined panel is the most direct reference for the named build.

Portion and ordering considerations

Ten pieces provide a different portion from four or six nuggets, and the meal adds a side and drink. The homepage guide also lists standalone nugget portions at other piece counts, but each has a different price. Use the count in the product name when comparing offers, and check whether the offer is a bundle or an individual item.

If you are ordering for someone with an allergy, use the component statements for the nuggets and fries rather than the menu name alone. The fries have their own wheat and milk declarations. A drink substitution or sauce can bring additional ingredients. The allergen table below summarizes the standard listed combination and does not promise that there is no shared kitchen contact.

Ingredients and how the item is built

The standard meal combines breaded Chicken McNuggets, potatoes prepared as fries and a medium fountain drink. The official meal page identifies the drink used for the calorie panel as Coca-Cola. The nuggets are made with white boneless chicken and a seasoned wheat flour coating. Fries have their own ingredient statement, including potatoes, vegetable oil, dextrose and salt.

A dipping sauce is not included in the standard meal components shown on the official page. If you add one, use that sauce’s own ingredient and nutrition details. The same applies if you replace the soft drink or change the fry size. A menu photo or bundle name alone cannot confirm the ingredients of a customized order.

Allergen information

The official component statements list wheat for the nuggets and wheat and milk for the fries. The Coca-Cola ingredient statement does not add a listed major allergen. A different drink or sauce can change the picture. Not listed is a description of the reviewed declaration, not an allergen free assurance, because McDonald’s warns that shared preparation can cause contact.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers on price, calories, ingredients and availability.

What is the McDonald’s 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal price?+

The homepage guide lists $10.09 for the meal. Local prices and participation can vary, so check the restaurant menu before ordering.

How many calories are in the 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets Meal?+

The official standard build has 1,000 calories with medium fries and a medium Coca-Cola, before adding a dipping sauce.

What comes with the 10 Piece McNuggets Meal?+

The standard build includes ten Chicken McNuggets, medium World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink. The official nutrition panel uses Coca-Cola.

How many calories are in 10 Piece Chicken McNuggets without the meal?+

Do sauces add calories to the meal?+

Yes. McDonald’s says dipping sauces add 30 to 110 calories each, depending on the sauce.
